## About

**Society . E X E** is the engineering practice of **Ahiézer Rada**, focused on banking security, cryptographic architecture, and cybersecurity infrastructure for the financial sector. Our work is grounded in direct, hands-on experience with payment terminals, **ISO 8583** messaging, **EMV** specifications, and the **DUKPT** and **EFTSec** standards that protect today's banking transactions.

## OTP-X — our flagship initiative

**OTP-X** is a one-time authentication proposal built to close the structural gaps that leave OTP, TOTP, and HOTP exposed to modern fraud — phishing, SIM swapping, replay attacks, OTP-injection bots, and malware-assisted social engineering.

Where classic schemes only confirm that a code is *correct*, OTP-X adds a layer of **access-coherence verification**, conceptually grounded in cryptographic standards already proven across the payments industry — DUKPT and EFTSec.

> ⚠️ **Current status — Phase 0.** OTP-X is in technical and legal definition. It holds no certification, independent evaluation, or regulatory approval. Nothing published here should be read as authorization for its use, integration, or implementation. More detail at **[society-exe.site/status](https://society-exe.site/status)**.
